# Code of conduct

Proofing Gallery welcomes participation regardless of experience, identity,
background, or ability. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable
technical feedback, and keep discussions focused on the project. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of another
person's private information are not acceptable.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these
expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report
conduct concerns privately through GitHub's maintainer contact mechanisms. Do
not use a public issue when doing so would expose a person or sensitive context.
